Privacy Policy — FB Warehouse

Application: FB Warehouse (package uz.fbwarehouse.wms) · Last updated: July 3, 2026

FB Warehouse is an internal warehouse management application intended solely for employees of FB Distributors. It is not a consumer application and requires a corporate account issued by the company.

Information we collect

  • Account credentials — a username and password issued by the company, used only to sign in to the corporate warehouse system.
  • User identifier and role — used to attribute warehouse operations (picking, receiving, inventory) performed by the employee.
  • Device push token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver work-related notifications (for example, new picking tasks).

The application does not collect location data, contacts, photos, files, advertising identifiers, or any data unrelated to warehouse operations. The camera is used only to scan product and box barcodes; images are processed on the device and are not stored or transmitted.

How we use information

Collected information is used exclusively to operate the company's warehouse management system: authentication, task assignment, stock operations and audit of employee actions required by internal policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Data is stored on servers controlled by FB Distributors and is accessible only to authorized company personnel.

Security

All communication between the application and the server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Access to data is restricted by role-based permissions.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained while the employee's account is active. When an employee leaves the company, the account is deactivated. To request deletion of your account data, contact the administrator at the e-mail address below.

Children

The application is intended for adult employees (18+) and is not directed at children.
